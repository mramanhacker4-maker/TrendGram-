Privacy Policy for TrendGram

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Welcome to TrendGram ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when using the TrendGram Android application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: User handle, username, and profile details provided during account creation or interaction.
  • User-Generated Content: Videos, captions, audio selections, comments, and community tags you publish within the application.
  • Watch & Reward Activity: Video watch duration, completed sessions, and wallet reward transactions to prevent fraudulent multi-claiming and ensure one-person unique view rewards.
  • Device & Usage Data: Basic technical device model, Android OS version, and network connectivity state for multimedia playback optimization.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the TrendGram short video streaming experience.
  • To process algorithmic boosts, creator monetization, and wallet cashout requests.
  • To prevent abuse, botting, and duplicate reward exploitation (enforcing one-person unique view tracking).
  • To deliver relevant community feeds (Entrepreneurship, Tech, Comedy, Travel, Fitness, Food).

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. All video streaming and media transfer occurs through secure HTTPS encryption. Your financial withdrawal details (UPI IDs) are processed solely for reward payout fulfillment.

4. Media Permissions

TrendGram uses the standard Android Photo and Video Picker (zero storage permissions required) allowing you to select and upload videos from your gallery securely without granting full access to your device files.

5. Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
